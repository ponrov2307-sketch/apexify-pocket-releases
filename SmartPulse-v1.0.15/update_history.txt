v1.0.15
Fixes the bug that made WiFi updates silently do nothing on the Lite.
The crash recorder wrote to RTC word 0 - the same word the bootloader
reads its "install the staged image" order from - so eboot failed its
CRC check and booted the old firmware. Upload said 200 OK and installed
nothing, seven times running. Moved to word 32.

Product renamed to SmartPulse Lite / SmartPulse Pro.
Pro: per-screen selection for AUTO rotation, and the Claude usage,
Codex and PC Monitor screens no longer render at the wrong scale.
